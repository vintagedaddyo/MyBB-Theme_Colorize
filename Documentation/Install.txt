
1.) After downloading the Theme unpack it (with 7-Zip for example)

2.) Upload the contents of "Upload" into your forums main directory.

3.) Go to the Admin Control Panel under Themes > Import

4.) Search for the XML File titled “Colorize-theme.xml" in the theme folder found on your computer and click the Button "Import Theme" this will upload the file from your computer to your forum.

5.) Now have fun with your forum!
